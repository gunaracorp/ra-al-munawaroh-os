## Content workflow
1. Draft oleh pemilik halaman.
2. Verifikasi fakta dan sumber.
3. Review privasi/safeguarding jika menyebut anak, foto atau informasi sensitif.
4. Persetujuan pejabat yang ditunjuk.
5. Publikasi dengan tanggal dan versi.
6. Review berkala dan penarikan konten usang.

## Fields for every page
- Page ID and route
- Intended audience and purpose
- Primary message and CTA
- Approved copy and source of truth
- Owner, reviewer, approver
- Privacy/safeguarding review needed?
- Last verified date and next review date
- Publication status: DRAFT / IN_REVIEW / APPROVED / PUBLISHED / RETIRED

## Rule
No invented address, phone, email, tuition, operating hours, enrollment period, accreditation, official status, or outcome statistics. No identifying child data or images without required permission and policy approval.
