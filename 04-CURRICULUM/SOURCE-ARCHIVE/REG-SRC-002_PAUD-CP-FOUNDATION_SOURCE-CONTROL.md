The official document title explicitly covers TK/RA/BA, KB, SPS, and TPA. Its indexed text states that educators should read the CP document as a whole, including rationale, objectives, learning characteristics, CP scope, and later phases because the phases are connected. It frames CP as curriculum input for PAUD units to design learning and provide appropriate stimulation through enriched environments and interactions with children and families.

5. Compare extracted text to the rendered PDF pages; correct OCR errors.
6. Record page numbers for each element and calculate SHA-256 of the source PDF.

8. Only then mark the CP source as LOCKED; use the exact official wording in the crosswalk, and keep all local interpretations clearly labelled as internal design.
