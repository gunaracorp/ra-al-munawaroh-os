## 1. Purpose and limitations

This document distinguishes findings supported by official public sources from details that remain unverified because a complete, machine-readable copy of both decisions and their attachments has not yet been archived and compared line by line.

KMA 1503/2025 is formally titled as an amendment to KMA 450/2024 on curriculum implementation for RA, MI, MTs, MA, and MAK. An official Ditjen Pendidikan Islam letter dated 20 October 2025 states that the decision emphasizes Pembelajaran Mendalam and Kurikulum Berbasis Cinta and directs socialization, integration into coaching/supervision/competency development, and periodic implementation reporting.

Official corroborating Kemenag coverage reports that the new decision discusses curriculum implementation, learning and assessment, and co-curricular guidance; a Kemenag regional report describes a terminology change from P5RA to Kokurikuler and more detailed curriculum structure. These are secondary official summaries, not substitutes for the operative decision text.
