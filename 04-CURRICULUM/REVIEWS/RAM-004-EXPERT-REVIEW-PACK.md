## 3. Reviewer eligibility and independence

The reviewer should ideally have one or more of:
- current experience as a Pengawas RA or official madrasah curriculum supervisor;
- relevant training/qualification in PAUD/RA curriculum, learning, and assessment;
- demonstrable experience with KMA 450/2024 and KMA 1503/2025 implementation;
- ability to identify provisions that apply specifically to RA rather than transfer assumptions from MI/MTs/MA.

Record reviewer identity and role only with their consent and according to project privacy policy. Do not invent a reviewer or fabricate a review.

## 4. Review checklist

- [ ] Full KMA 1503/2025 and all attachments are archived and integrity checked.
- [ ] KMA 450/2024 baseline is archived and compared clause-by-clause.
- [ ] Every amendment/replacement has exact page/clause evidence.
- [ ] Applicability to RA is explicit for every mapped requirement.
- [ ] Current official CP PAUD version is verified against the official repository.
- [ ] Every CP element has verbatim official text, page citation and source metadata.
- [ ] Internal competency/outcome language is distinguished from official CP.
- [ ] No premature academic drilling or rigid age-based developmental claims.
- [ ] Islamic practice and values are expressed accurately, age-appropriately and with respect for child dignity.
- [ ] Assessment is formative, authentic, inclusive and privacy-aware.
- [ ] Required changes have owners and due dates.
